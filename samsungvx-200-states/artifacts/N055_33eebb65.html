  • On Timer

The VXT Player app is automatically updated when the screens are turned off and then turned back on. Set the On Timer and Off Timer to ensure the VXT Player app stays up to date.

Two simple ways to set the On Timer and Off Timer
Go to the Screen menu and select Energy Management, or set the profile in the Settings > Preset menu.
